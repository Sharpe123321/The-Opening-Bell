      Why this exists

      When I applied to business school, grades were nearly all that mattered. That has changed. Programs like Ivey, Rotman, Queen's and Waterloo now look closely at what you've done outside class.

      The opportunities exist, but the dates are scattered and the important steps are buried. This site puts them in one place, for the students who are where I was.

      How we check dates

      Every listing is checked against the organizer's own website, and shows the date we checked it and a link to the source.

      A date marked confirmed was published by the organizer for this season. A date marked est. is our estimate from past years, so a closed listing still tells you when to come back.

      Your privacy

      We don't collect anything. No accounts, no email, no tracking cookies.

      Your grade choice is saved only in your own browser. "Add to calendar" downloads a file straight to your device; nothing is sent to us.

      Grades mean your grade in the 2026–27 school year. For next year's rounds (like Wharton), we list the grades that will still be eligible when they open.

      Always confirm details on the organizer's site before you apply. Opening Bell is independent and isn't affiliated with any organizer listed.
